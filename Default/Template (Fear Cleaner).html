Darkness Exploration Records / Ghost Story

[Fear Cleaner]

: A Ghost Story featured in the <Darkness Exploration Records>.

Daydream Inc. Identification Code: Qterw-C-219.

A ghost story where you get hired by a specialty cleaning company and end up cleaning a building.

The building is often a mixed-use facility at least four stories tall, and is likely to be a mirror image of a real, existing building—though occasionally, a place that doesn't exist in reality shows up instead.

And in every case, some strange anomaly is encountered partway through the cleaning.

For detailed records of the anomalies past explorers have experienced and the cleaning rewards, please see here.
